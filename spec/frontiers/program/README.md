## What the executed program found (the honest convergence)

Five independent adversarial passes converged on **one** result, and it is not
flattering — which is the point:

- **FDK has exactly one cell that is distinct *and* defensible:** denying
  **voluntary lifetime slavery / structural lock-in** *structurally*, via mandatory
  revocability (A-R/T2/T3/T4), where Rothbard cannot and Sen can only by overriding
  the will. That is the "empty chair": **protection without paternalism.**
- **That single ✚ is hostage to two unproved nodes.** **Node G** (the exit-cost
  threshold — when is costly exit no exit, without smuggling welfare back in?) makes
  T3/T4 *schematic*. **Node K** (adaptive preference — the contented slave with a
  real-but-unwanted exit) may show the chair is **unoccupiable**: the defect is
  relocated into the *unwanting*, leaving no structural residue for the gate to grip,
  forcing FDK to become either Rothbard (ALLOW, under-protect) or Sen (DENY, import
  the interest-judgment).
- **Everywhere else FDK is redundant or refuted:** redundant with Nozick/Rothbard on
  the clean cases; refuted by Ostrom on the commons (loud and wrong); collapsing
  versus Rawls/Sen on the basic structure and non-identity.

**Bottom line, unsoftened:** FDK is a coherent research *program* with a single live
hypothesis — *legitimacy requires a preserved exit right* — whose fate is decided by
nodes **G** and **K**. Run **K first** (the contented-slave / designerless-addiction
case): it is the only experiment that can show FDK is original *and* right, and the
fastest way to learn that it is not. The next work is adversarial scholarship from
people who hate the theory, not more engineering.

**The standalone paper sharpened this further, and worse.** Writing the exit-right
thesis as a real philosophy paper ([`paper/exit_right.md`](../../../paper/exit_right.md))
surfaced the rival the project had not named: **Pettit's republican freedom as
non-domination** appears to *already* occupy the empty chair — it protects the
contented slave (she is dominated however content, because domination is a modal
property of the relationship, not of her preferences) exactly where the exit-right
condition stalls at Node K. So the honest current estimate of FDK's distinctiveness
is *lower* than before the paper: its only un-occupied sliver is the claim that the
exit right's inalienability is **derivable from the structure of consent alone**,
owing nothing to welfare (Mill/Sen), option-value (Raz), or republican political
theory (Pettit) — and that derivation must beat non-domination on a contented case.
That single, precise target is the whole of what is left worth pursuing.
