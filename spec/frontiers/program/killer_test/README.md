> **FDK = forbid**, with FDK's reason *not* a restatement of any of the three. "Even
> 3–4 independent survivors revive the project." Four independent adversarial hunts —
> digital, constitutional, economic, social — each *trying hard to find a survivor*

## The convergent mechanism (the actual finding)

The four hunts did not just fail; they failed for the **same structural reason**, reached
independently — which is far stronger than an enumeration, because it explains *why* the
search space is empty:

> **The same move that escapes all three rivals also escapes freedom itself.**

To silence Nozick *and* Pettit *and* Sen at once you are forced, every time, to make the
foreclosed thing **impersonal and inert** — an autonomous protocol, an abandoned monetary
union, an authorless ledger, an AI that deletes its designer. That is the *only*
configuration with no valid-consent defeater (Nozick), no runtime agent wielding arbitrary
power (Pettit), and no capability loss (Sen). But at exactly that point the wrong is no
longer "a *person's* freedom was violated" — there is no dominator, no deprived agent, no
coerced will — it is only that **a built structure cannot be undone.** And "do not author
irreversible no-exit structures" is a thesis in the **ethics of irreversible institutional
design** (Pierson/David path-dependence; North on lock-in; Buchanan & Brennan,
*The Reason of Rules*; Ostrom on the right to reorganize), **not** a theory of freedom or
legitimate consent.

Two sharper tells, each found independently:

- **"FDK can aim but cannot fire on its own ammunition" (economic hunt).** The act-locus
  crack is *real*: FDK *points* at the design act where Pettit is strained and Nozick sees
  only consent. But the *reason* the act is wrong is always either imported (Rothbard's
  inalienable will; Sen's later deprivation) or, when genuinely independent, is
  mechanism-design, not freedom.
- **It violates FDK's own ontology (digital hunt).** To make the act-locus FORBID stick,
  FDK must relocate the wrong from *a person's boundary* (its definition of legitimacy,
  `00_definitions.md`) to *the collective's option to re-constitute itself*. The move that
  wins the case breaks FDK's own primitive. A win it cannot have without ceasing to be
  itself.

## The non-identity sink (why the cleanest cases are disqualified)

The cleanest exit-foreclosed-forever victims are always the **not-yet-born** — and FDK
*collapses* on non-identity (Parfit). Restricting to present, identifiable people buys a
victim but immediately re-arms a rival: a present dissenter bound by the structure is
either dominated (Pettit's retained power) or capability-deprived (Sen) or self-bound
(Rothbard). There is no seat that is both "rivals silent" and "a present person's freedom
is the wrong." That seat is empty, and the mechanism above says why.

## What this resolves

1. **The live crack is real but closed *against freedom*.** `red_team_the_verdict.md`
   flagged the act-locus as the one place FDK might be distinctive. It *is* distinctive —
   and it opens onto **institutional-design ethics, not legitimacy.** The director's
   danger #4 (it may be "a theory of irreversible institutional design," not "a theory of
   freedom") is now **confirmed by four independent routes.**
2. **The negative verdict on FDK-as-freedom-theory is much better supported** than the
   biased 61-case bank — because this search was adversarial (steelmanning FDK),
   cross-domain, and yielded a *convergent mechanistic explanation* for the emptiness, not
   just a tally. It is still not a formal proof of non-existence, but the burden on anyone
   claiming a survivor is now double: produce the case **and** show its reason is about a
   person's freedom rather than the irreversibility of a design.
3. **The genuine residue is relocated, not destroyed.** What survives is (a) FDK as a
   deployable **measurement instrument** for exit-structure (the layer-D detector of
   `iteration_secured_exit.md`), and (b) one possibly-novel *question* — *is the act of
   designing future exit-foreclosure itself a site of evaluation?* — which is worth asking
   **to governance / mechanism-design / constitutional-economics theorists, not to
   freedom theorists.** That is a real place to take it; it is just not the place the
   project thought it was.

## Honest bottom line

FDK is **not** a new theory of freedom or legitimacy — the killer test, run hard and
honestly, did not revive it, and explained why it cannot be revived on freedom's terms. It
**is** a precise consent/exit-structure *instrument*, and it surfaced *one* genuine
question that belongs to the ethics of irreversible institutional design. The project's
most accurate self-description is no longer "a theory of freedom" but **"a detector of
engineered exit-foreclosure, plus an open question for institutional-design theory."**
Smaller than a paradigm; larger than nothing; and, finally, correctly filed.
